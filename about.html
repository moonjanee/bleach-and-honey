// ARTIST STATEMENT

BLEACHJANE

Visual artist & prospective tattoo apprentice based in South Africa.

Bleach & Honey represents an exploration between opposing textures; raw fine-line graphite, dark flash imagery, organic figure studies and chrome line work.

Focused on fine-line ink, classical anatomy and custom flash sheets. Each piece balances delicate line work with stark and tactile contrast.

Exploring dark flash, fine-line graphite and anatomy studies with the goal of joining a shop as an apprentice.
